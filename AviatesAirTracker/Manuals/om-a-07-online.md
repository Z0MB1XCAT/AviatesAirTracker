Flying online is encouraged, and it's what makes our events come alive. On a network you share the sky with real people, so **the network's rules come first**: the VATSIM Code of Conduct or the IVAO rules, and the controller's instructions.

## Before you connect

- File a **flight plan** that matches your OFP: route, cruise level, aircraft type with equipment and wake category, and your alternate. SimBrief can prefile it for you.
- **Callsign:** your booking callsign (VAV / VAH / VAC + suffix; see [OM-A 1](doc:om-a-01#callsigns-and-flight-numbers)).
- **Remarks:** include `/V/` (voice) and `RMK/AVIATESAIR VIRTUAL`, plus anything ATC should know (e.g. `RMK/NEW TO THE AREA`). If ATC asks how to say your callsign, spell out the designator and suffix.
- Connect **at the gate**, never on a runway or taxiway. Set your transponder to standby or as the network requires.

## On the frequency

- **Listen before you talk.** Plan the call, then make it in one go.
- Use standard **ICAO phraseology** and read back clearances, runway, altitude, heading, speed and squawk.
- If you don't understand, say so: "*Say again*". Nobody minds.
- Check in with **callsign, level and anything asked for** (the ATIS letter, SID or STAR).

## When there's no ATC

Monitor and self-announce on **UNICOM 122.800** (VATSIM) or the network's published unicom. Give position and intentions at key points: pushback, taxi, lining up, departing, established on final, vacating.

## Datalink

- Where the controller provides it, request **departure clearance (PDC/DCL)** and use **CPDLC** logon via Hoppie's ACARS.
- AviatesAir's own ACARS messages (loadsheet, timings) come through Hoppies when it is enabled in the Tracker; they're company messages, not ATC clearances.

## Events

- For events, **book early**, arrive at the gate in good time and **carry extra fuel** for holding.
- Keep speeds as assigned. On a busy frequency, keep transmissions short and vacate promptly after landing.
- Confirm attendance at the event reminder so the event's hours multiplier is applied (see [OM-A 1](doc:om-a-01#bookings)).

## Non-normals online

- You may simulate a non-normal only if you can **fly it realistically without disrupting other traffic**. On a busy event, don't.
- Tell ATC early and plainly what you need ("*…request return to the field, engine failure, VAV12 heavy*"). Don't surprise them with a squawk change.
- **Never squawk 7500** (unlawful interference) on any network.
- If the simulator misbehaves (a crash, or a freeze on final), leave the frequency and **disconnect** rather than stay connected as a hazard.
