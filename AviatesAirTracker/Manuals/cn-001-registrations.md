> **NOTE** Status: **ACTIVE** until cancelled. Applies to every AviatesAir, CityHopper and Skycargo type.

## Notice

AviatesAir does not assign airframes to individual pilots or stations. You may fly **any registration in the AviatesAir fleet**:

- from **any airport** in the network,
- **as many times as you like**, and
- **even if another pilot is flying the same registration at the same time.**

Maintaining a unique registration for every position in a shared virtual fleet would add administration without adding anything to the operation, so the whole fleet is pooled.

## The one condition: routes for your type

Your departure airport must have AviatesAir **routes for the aircraft type you intend to fly**. Before booking, check the route list in the Tracker (**Routes**) for your departure airport and filter by type.

> **NOTE** Example: East Midlands (EGNX) has Boeing 737-800 routes, so a B738 departure from EGNX with any B738 registration is valid.

If the departure airport has no routes for your type, choose another type that is served there, or reposition to an airport that has routes for the type you want (see [OM-A 1, Repositioning](doc:om-a-01#repositioning)).

## What does not change

- Your **booking** still defines the flight: route, flight number, aircraft **type** and schedule. The registration you pick must be of the booked type.
- File the registration you are actually flying in your SimBrief OFP and your network flight plan so ATC, the OFP and the Tracker agree.
- Company callsigns are unaffected. See [OM-A 1, Callsigns](doc:om-a-01#callsigns-and-flight-numbers).
