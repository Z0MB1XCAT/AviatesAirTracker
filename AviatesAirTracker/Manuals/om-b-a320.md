## Configuration policy

- **Takeoff:** CONF 1+F, 2 or 3 from EFB Departure Perf (OPT recommended). FLEX when performance allows.
- **Landing:** **CONF FULL standard; CONF 3 minimum** ([CN-003](doc:cn-003)). Choose CONF 3 for a reason (windshear expected, go-around performance) and brief it.
- **Autobrake:** LO for normal landings on long dry runways, MED for short or wet runways.

## Handling notes

- **Managed** speed and descent are normal. Monitor the energy circle and the descent profile, and use speedbrakes early rather than late.
- **Approach speed:** VAPP = VLS + the greater of 5 kt (A/THR ON) and ⅓ of the headwind component, 15 kt maximum. EFB Arrival Perf computes it.
- **Flare:** at about 30 ft. "RETARD" is called at 20 ft: bring the thrust levers to idle. Avoid holding the aircraft off; the A320 family floats readily.
- **Tail-strike awareness:** don't over-rotate. Target about 15° after lift-off at 3° per second.
- **neo:** allow for the longer engine warm-up before takeoff thrust and the cool-down before shutdown that the add-on's procedures call for.
