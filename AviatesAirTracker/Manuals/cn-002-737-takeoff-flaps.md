> **WARNING** Status: **ACTIVE** until cancelled. Applies to the **B736, B738, B739ER and B38M**.

## Restriction

**FLAPS 5 is the minimum flap setting for takeoff.** FLAPS 1 is **not permitted**, even when the runway, weight and weather would make it performance-legal.

Use FLAPS 5 as the normal setting and FLAPS 10, 15 or 25 when performance requires more flap (short or limiting runways, obstacles, high density altitude).

## Why

A lower flap setting means higher rotation and lift-off speeds and a **higher body attitude at lift-off**, which reduces tail clearance. The 737-800, -900ER and MAX 8 are long-bodied and particularly sensitive to this.

A tail strike is never a small event: the aircraft must **return to the departure airport** for inspection. That means a long delay to your flight, disruption to the rest of the rotation and the risk of further problems with the airframe later.

## How the tools apply it

- **EFB Departure Perf** only offers FLAPS 5 and above for 737 types, and **OPT** only chooses between permitted settings. If a restricted setting is ever requested, the result is flagged with this notice.
- Rotate at the normal rate (about 2–3° per second) towards the target pitch attitude. Don't rotate early or aggressively. See [OM-B 737, Handling notes](doc:om-b-737#handling-notes).
- A tail strike, or a takeoff flown with a prohibited setting, is a reportable occurrence. Tell staff via the PIREP review comment (see [OM-A 6](doc:om-a-06#held-for-review)).
