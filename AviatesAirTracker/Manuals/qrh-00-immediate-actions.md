> **WARNING** Reference only. The **ECAM / QRH of your aircraft add-on** is the authority for your type. Fly the aircraft first; do the memory items from memory, then complete the checklist.

## Rejected takeoff

| | Action |
|---|---|
| PF calls | **"STOP"** |
| Thrust levers | **IDLE / CLOSE** (autothrottle disconnects) |
| Reverse thrust | **MAX** as required |
| Brakes | **RTO autobrake**, or maximum manual braking |
| Speedbrake / ground spoilers | Check **deployed** |
| Direction | Maintain the **centreline** |
| After stopping | Parking brake **SET**. Notify ATC and the cabin, then assess (fire? evacuation?) |

## Engine fire or severe damage

**Airbus** (ECAM ENG FIRE): thrust lever (affected) **IDLE**; engine master (affected) **OFF**; fire push-button **PUSH**; agent 1 **DISCH** after 10 s; agent 2 if the fire persists after 30 s.

**Boeing** (ENG FIRE): autothrottle **DISENGAGE**; thrust lever (affected) **CLOSE**; engine start lever / fuel control switch (affected) **CUTOFF**; engine fire switch **PULL, then ROTATE** to the stop to discharge; if the fire warning persists after 30 seconds, rotate it to the other bottle.

## Emergency descent

1. Crew oxygen masks **ON, 100 %**. Establish communication.
2. Signs **ON**; passenger oxygen **ON** if the cabin altitude exceeds 14,000 ft.
3. Descent **INITIATE**: altitude selector to the lowest safe altitude (MEA/MORA, typically FL100), heading as required, speed **MAX / appropriate**, speedbrakes **FULL**.
4. Transponder **7700** unless ATC has assigned another code. Declare **MAYDAY**.

## TCAS resolution advisory

- **Follow the RA** promptly and smoothly: disconnect the autopilot and flight directors and fly the green band. On Airbus types with the AP/FD TCAS mode, the autopilot may fly the RA; monitor it.
- **Never** manoeuvre against the RA, even if ATC has given a conflicting instruction.
- Report "**TCAS RA**" to ATC as soon as possible. When clear of conflict, return to the clearance and report "**clear of conflict, returning to…**".

## Terrain warning: "PULL UP" / "TERRAIN AHEAD PULL UP"

- **Airbus:** AP **OFF**; pitch **pull to full back stick**; thrust **TOGA**; speedbrakes **RETRACT**; wings **LEVEL**.
- **Boeing:** AP and A/T **DISENGAGE**; thrust **MAX**; pitch to **20°** and roll wings level; speedbrakes **RETRACT**. Stay at the pitch limit indication if needed.
- Climb until the warning stops **and** you are safe. Do not change configuration until then.

## Windshear

- **Takeoff roll** (before V1): reject if there's enough runway, otherwise continue.
- **Airborne / on approach** ("WINDSHEAR"): thrust **TOGA / MAX**; follow the flight director or pitch to about 15° (Boeing: up to the pitch limit indicator); **do not change the configuration** (gear or flaps) until out of the shear; speedbrakes **RETRACT**.
- **Predictive "WINDSHEAR AHEAD"** on approach: **go around**.

## Unreliable airspeed

If a flight path is in danger (Airbus memory items): AP / FD / A/THR **OFF**, then set pitch and thrust, keep the flaps where they are, retract the speedbrakes, and raise the gear after takeoff.

| Phase | Pitch | Thrust |
|---|---|---|
| Below the thrust-reduction altitude | **15°** | **TOGA** |
| Above the thrust-reduction altitude, below FL100 | **10°** | **CLB** |
| Above FL100 | **5°** | **CLB** |

Boeing: A/T and AP **DISENGAGE**, flight director switches **OFF**, then set the attitude and thrust for the flight phase from the QRH table.

## Stall recovery

1. **Nose down:** reduce the angle of attack, push to unload (trim nose-down as needed).
2. **Bank:** roll **wings level**.
3. **Thrust:** as needed; mind the pitch-up couple of underwing engines.
4. **Speedbrakes:** retract.
5. Return to the desired flight path when out of the stall, without triggering a secondary stall.
