## Airport and ground requirements

- The A380 needs **Code F** taxiways, stands and runways. Plan only to airports with A380 routes; they're approved for it.
- Expect **longer pushback and taxi times** and follow-me vehicles at some airports.
- Use the gate the airport scenery designates for the A380. Upper-deck boarding bridges are needed for a realistic turnaround.

## Wake turbulence

- File and fly as **"Super"** on the network. Aircraft behind you need enlarged separation: up to 8 NM for a light aircraft on approach.
- Departing behind another A380, allow at least **3 minutes** unless ATC applies radar separation.

## Configuration policy

- **Takeoff:** CONF 1+F, 2 or 3 per performance, with FLEX.
- **Landing:** **CONF FULL standard**. **BTV** is preferred where the exit is known; otherwise autobrake per the runway state.

## Handling notes

- **Inertia:** plan speed and altitude changes well ahead. The aircraft decelerates slowly in level flight.
- **Flare** at about 40–50 ft with a small pitch change and progressive thrust reduction. Touch down on the main gear and lower the nose promptly.
- **Taxi** with the external taxi camera system. Keep the nose wheel on the centreline in turns and watch for wingtip clearance.
