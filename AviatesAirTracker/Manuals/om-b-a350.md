## Configuration policy

- **Takeoff:** CONF 1+F, 2 or 3 per performance. FLEX is normal on long-haul departures. Heavy departures commonly need CONF 2 or 3.
- **Landing:** **CONF FULL standard**; CONF 3 when briefed (windshear, go-around performance).
- **Braking:** **BTV** (brake-to-vacate) is preferred where the runway exit is known: select the exit in the approach briefing. Otherwise use autobrake LO/2/3 as the runway requires.

## Handling notes

- **Pitch trim** is set automatically on the ground, so there's no trim setting in the checklist. Check the takeoff data instead.
- **Long haul:** plan crew rest and fuel checks at every ETOPS entry point and waypoint. Keep the step-climb plan from the OFP.
- **Descent planning:** allow for a long, shallow deceleration. Start the descent on the managed profile and use speedbrakes early rather than late.
- **Flare** at about 30 ft, idle by touchdown. The A350 is stable in the flare, so avoid the tendency to hold it off.
