## Configuration policy

- **Takeoff:** **FLAPS 5 standard and minimum.** FLAPS 10, 15 or 25 for limiting runways. **FLAPS 1 is not permitted** ([CN-002](doc:cn-002)).
- **Landing:** **FLAPS 30 standard; FLAPS 25 minimum**; FLAPS 40 for short, wet or contaminated runways ([CN-003](doc:cn-003)).
- **Autobrake:** RTO for takeoff; 2 for normal landings, 3 for wet or shorter runways, MAX for contaminated runways.
- **Speedbrake:** ARMED for landing; check that it deploys on touchdown.

## Handling notes

- **Rotation:** a smooth 2–3° per second towards about 15° nose-up. The longer the fuselage, the smaller the **tail-strike margin**: the 737-800, MAX 8 and especially the -900ER have far less than the -600. Never rotate before VR, and never rotate rapidly.
- **Approach speed:** VREF + ½ the steady headwind + the full gust increment, minimum +5 kt, maximum +20 kt.
- **Flare:** begin at about 20 ft, raise the nose 2–3° and retard the thrust to idle. **Don't hold it off.** The 737 lands best firmly and on speed, and floating risks a tail strike on the -900ER.
- **Pitch at touchdown:** keep the normal landing attitude; don't push the nose down in the flare (a flat or nose-low touchdown risks a nose-gear-first landing), and never raise it to stretch the float.
- **MAX 8:** probe heat stays in **AUTO** (see the checklist). The LEAP-1B needs a longer warm-up before takeoff thrust.
