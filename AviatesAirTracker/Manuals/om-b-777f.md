## Cargo operations

- **Loadsheet:** check the OFP's zero-fuel weight and the loadsheet MACZFW / MACTOW against the EFB (Pilot Brief → Loadsheet) before computing takeoff performance. Late changes of more than 1,000 kg need a new performance calculation.
- **Payload** is in kilograms on the loadsheet. Enter takeoff weight in tonnes in Departure Perf.
- **Supernumeraries:** couriers and animal handlers travel in the upper-deck area and receive a safety briefing.
- Only dangerous goods listed on the **NOTOC** (notice to captain) are carried. Brief their location and the fire drill for the main deck.

## Configuration policy

- **Takeoff:** FLAPS 5, 15 or 20 per performance. Heavy freighter departures often need 15 or 20. Use derate or assumed temperature where performance allows.
- **Landing:** **FLAPS 30 standard**; FLAPS 25 when briefed.
- **Autobrake:** RTO for takeoff; 2 or 3 for landing as the runway requires.

## Handling notes

- **Rotation:** a steady 2–2.5° per second. The long fuselage has a limited tail-strike margin, and the tail skid gives an early warning.
- **Approach:** the 777 is stable but slow to decelerate. Be configured and on speed by the 1,000 ft gate at heavy weights.
- **Flare** at about 30 ft, retard at about 25 ft, and don't float.
