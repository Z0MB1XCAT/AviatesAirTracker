## Configuration policy

- **Takeoff:** FLAPS 10 or 20 per performance, with assumed temperature or derate where available.
- **Landing:** **FLAPS 30 standard**; FLAPS 25 when briefed (for example for go-around performance at high weights).
- **Autobrake:** RTO for takeoff; 2, 3 or 4 for landing as the runway requires.

## Handling notes

- **Four engines:** set takeoff thrust symmetrically and monitor all four through the takeoff roll. An engine failure after V1 needs less rudder than on a twin, but still needs prompt, accurate rudder.
- **Rotation:** about 2–3° per second to the target attitude. Mind the tail on the long-bodied -8.
- **Flare:** begin at about 30 ft, and reduce thrust to idle during the flare. Keep the wings level. The outboard engine pods sit low in a crosswind.
- **Taxi:** judge turns from the main-gear position, which is well behind you, and use oversteer at narrow taxiway junctions.
