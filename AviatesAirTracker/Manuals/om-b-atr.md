## Hotel mode and propeller brake

- **Engine 2** can run with the **propeller brake ON** (hotel mode) to provide electrical power and air conditioning on the ground, like an APU.
- Never approach the right-hand propeller while engine 2 is running in hotel mode. Treat it as a running engine.
- **Before taxi:** propeller brake **OFF** (the "unfeather" sequence), condition levers **AUTO**.
- **After landing:** taxi in on engine 2. Shut down engine 1 (CL FTR then FUEL SO) after at least **one minute** at idle.

## Icing

- The ATR operates at levels where icing is common. When the **ICING AOA** light is on, the icing speeds apply (the red bugs) and the **minimum manoeuvring speeds increase**.
- Engage the airframe de-icing boots at the first sign of ice accretion and keep them on until clear of icing conditions.
- In **severe icing** (ice on the side windows, unexpected roll or heavy vibration): leave the icing conditions immediately and disengage the autopilot while holding the controls firmly.

- Power management selector: **TO** for takeoff, **MCT** after an engine failure, **CLB** and **CRZ** as the phases require.
- TLU **LO SPD** for landing (check it has moved).

## Handling notes

- **Rotation** at VR to about 6–8°. The ATR 72 has a long fuselage, so don't over-rotate.
- **Approach:** a stable 3° path at VAPP, with power (not pitch) controlling speed. Keep a little power in the flare.
- **Flare** is small: reduce power progressively from about 20 ft and let the aircraft settle. Avoid pulling the power to flight idle high up, as the sink rate builds quickly.
- **Crosswind:** the ATR has a relatively low wing loading and a high wing. Hold aileron into wind during the takeoff and landing roll, and engage the gust lock only when parked.
- **Tail prop:** fit the **tail stand** on the ATR 72 whenever it is parked (it isn't needed on the ATR 42).
