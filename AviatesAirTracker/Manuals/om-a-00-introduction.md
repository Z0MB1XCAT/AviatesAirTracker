Welcome to AviatesAir. This Operations Manual sets out how we fly: the policies, procedures and standards every AviatesAir, CityHopper and Skycargo pilot follows. It is written the way a real airline writes its manuals, so that flying with us feels like flying for an airline, while staying practical for a virtual operation.

> **CAUTION** AviatesAir is a **virtual airline** for flight simulation. Nothing in this library is approved or certified data, and none of it may be used for real-world aviation.

## The document library

| Manual | Contents |
|---|---|
| **Company NOTAMs (CN)** | Current operational notices. Read and **acknowledge** every active notice before your next flight. |
| **OM-A: General / Basic** | Operating policy, flight planning, fuel, weather and minima, SOPs, approach and landing policy, flight data monitoring, online operations, abnormal and emergency policy. |
| **OM-B: Aircraft Operating Matters** | One chapter per fleet: key data and limitations, company configuration policy, handling notes. |
| **QRH: Quick Reference Handbook** | Normal checklists for every type (identical to the EFB **Checklists** app) and immediate-action items. |
| **Pilot Guides** | The AviatesAir Tracker, ACARS and EFB, plus weather and performance know-how. |

## Order of precedence

When two documents disagree, apply the **first** in this list:

1. **ATC instructions** and the safety of the flight (in the simulator)
2. **Company NOTAMs**: they amend the manuals until the manuals are revised
3. **OM-B** for your aircraft type
4. **OM-A**
5. The aircraft add-on's own documentation (FCOM/QRH/FCTM supplied with it)
6. Pilot Guides

> **NOTE** Aircraft data in OM-B are typical figures for the type. Where your add-on's own limitations differ (engine option, weight variant, software standard), **the add-on's figures apply**.

## Terminology

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **shall / must** | Mandatory. Deviations may lead to a PIREP being held for review. |
| **should** | Recommended practice. Deviate only with good reason, and brief it. |
| **may** | Optional; at the pilot's discretion. |
| **PF / PM** | Pilot Flying / Pilot Monitoring. Flying alone you do both, but think in both roles. |
| **AAL / AGL** | Above aerodrome level / above ground level. |

## Revisions

Every document shows its **revision number** and **effective date**. When a document is revised, its revision number increases. A revised Company NOTAM must be acknowledged again. Suggestions and corrections are welcome: raise them with AviatesAir staff on Discord.
