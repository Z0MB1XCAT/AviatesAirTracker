## Company configuration policy

| Type | Takeoff flap | Landing flap: standard | Landing flap: minimum |
|---|---|---|---|
| A319 / A320neo | CONF 1+F, 2 or 3 (OPT) | **CONF FULL** | **CONF 3** ([CN-003](doc:cn-003)) |
| A350 | CONF 1+F, 2 or 3 | CONF FULL | CONF 3 |
| A380 | CONF 1+F, 2 or 3 | CONF FULL | CONF 3 |
| 737 NG / MAX | **FLAPS 5 minimum** ([CN-002](doc:cn-002)); 10, 15, 25 as required | **FLAPS 30** | **FLAPS 25** ([CN-003](doc:cn-003)) |
| 777F | FLAPS 5, 15 or 20 | FLAPS 30 | FLAPS 25 |
| 747-8 | FLAPS 10 or 20 | FLAPS 30 | FLAPS 25 |
| ATR 72-600 | FLAPS 15 | FLAPS 30 | FLAPS 15 in icing / as required |
| ATR 42-600 | FLAPS 15 | FLAPS 35 | FLAPS 25 as required |

> **NOTE** Only the 737 and A320-family entries are company **restrictions** (Company NOTAMs). The others describe normal practice.
