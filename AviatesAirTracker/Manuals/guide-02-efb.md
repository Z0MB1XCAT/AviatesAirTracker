The EFB is the AviatesAir tablet. It runs on any device on your home network (iPad, Android tablet, phone or a second monitor) and is fed live by the Tracker.

## Pairing

1. In the Tracker, open **Settings → EFB Companion** and start the EFB server. Tick "Launch the EFB server when the app starts" to keep it on.
2. On your device (same Wi-Fi network), open the address shown, or scan the QR code.
3. Enter the **6-digit pairing code** if asked. The device stays paired.

> **NOTE** Add the EFB to your tablet's home screen for a full-screen, app-like experience. On the tablet, swipe up on the home bar or press **Esc** to return to the home screen.

## The apps

| App | What it does |
|---|---|
| **AviatesAir** | The flight dashboard: route and progress, OOOI times against the schedule, next waypoint and ETA, the vertical profile, live data, fuel and descent advisor, weather, current checklists, last landing. |
| **Departure Perf** | Takeoff performance: runway data, FLEX/TOGA, V-speeds, trim and flap (company flap restrictions applied), with the FMS takeoff page to copy. |
| **Arrival Perf** | Landing performance: runway condition (RWYCC), autobrake, reversers, failures, VAPP and landing distance. |
| **Map** | Moving map with your route, weather radar, SIGMETs, METARs, and VATSIM/IVAO traffic and ATC. |
| **Pilot Brief** | Your OFP: flight summary, fuel, weather, NOTAMs, loadsheet and the full plan. |
| **Weather** | Weather for your departure, arrival and alternate (or any ICAO you search). Shows the live VATSIM or IVAO ATIS with its letter and frequency, warns you when a new ATIS letter comes out, and lists the controllers online. Below that: the decoded METAR with a wind dial over the runways, the crosswind on every runway, the cloud layers, a TAF timeline with your ETA marked and any SIGMETs over the field. If no ATIS is online, one is built from the METAR. |
| **Checklists** | The electronic normal checklist for your booked aircraft (Airbus, Boeing or ATR style). |
| **Landing** | Your touchdown report: score, FDM events, approach profile, touchdown zone and stabilised-approach gates. |
| **Manuals** | This library: Company NOTAMs, OM-A, OM-B, QRH and guides, with search, bookmarks and PDF export. |
| **Messages** | Chat with other AviatesAir pilots, the same messages as the desktop app. The **Crew Room** goes to every pilot; everything else is one to one. Start a conversation from your pilots, from anyone **flying now**, or with a friend code (yours is at the bottom of the list). **Share my flight** drops your callsign, route, level and ETA into a message. A clock next to your message means it is queued and will go out when the desktop app next reaches the server. |
| **ACARS** | Datalink on the Hoppie network, under your own Hoppie logon code. Message company dispatch (VAOPS) or any station, request a pre-departure clearance, get a METAR, TAF or ATIS, and log on to a controller's CPDLC to send requests. Answer uplinks with **WILCO**, **UNABLE** or **STANDBY** on the message screen. Messages still reach your in-sim ACARS too. The **Ops log** shows the automatic company messages. |
| **Settings** | Theme, light or dark, **interface size** (Auto makes the EFB bigger on a large monitor), wallpaper, and **Units**: one weight setting, KG or LB, used by every app on every device. **Flight mode** turns alerts, sterile cockpit and keep screen on on or off for this device. Also the EFB-only SimBrief import, ending the flight, your datalink status, the devices connected and the pairing code for adding another. |
| **Scratchpad** | Your notepad: pages for the CRAFT clearance, ATIS, takeoff and approach briefs, holding, frequencies and taxi route, plus blank, lined, grid and typed pages. Write with a pen, highlighter or text stamps; values the EFB already knows appear as faint hints. **New flight** starts a fresh set of pages. |

## Flight mode

The EFB follows your flight by itself:

- **Passing 1,000 ft** on the climb it switches to flight mode. Below **1,000 ft** on the way down it switches to approach mode, and after touchdown it shows **Landed** with a link to your landing report.
- The **dock** at the bottom of the home screen changes with the phase: performance and the brief before departure, the map and weather in flight, checklists and arrival performance on the approach, the landing report after landing.
- **Sterile cockpit**: chat messages are held while you taxi, below 10,000 ft and on the approach. **STERILE** shows in the status bar with the number held, and they appear together once you're clear. ACARS and CPDLC always come through straight away.
- New messages and ACARS uplinks show as a banner at the top when you're in another app. Tap it to open the message.
- The screen stays on during a flight. On a tablet this starts after your next tap.

## Manuals app tips

- **Company NOTAMs** marked **READ & ACKNOWLEDGE** show a badge on the home screen until you acknowledge them.
- **Search** covers every manual; tap a result to jump straight to the section.
- Documents for **your aircraft** are pinned at the top of the library automatically.
- **Export PDF** prints the document, or a whole manual, to a PDF you can share.
